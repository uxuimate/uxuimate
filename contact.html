Common questions

Straight answers on process, payment, ownership, and what happens after launch.

What happens after I get in touch?

+
-

We reply within 24 hours. If it looks like a fit, we book a free 30-minute call to understand your business, goals, and what’s not working. After that you get a clear proposal — scope, timeline, and a fixed price — before any work starts.

How much does a project cost?

+
-

We give a fixed quote before any work starts, so there are no surprise invoices. Smaller website projects often start from around £1,499; larger custom work typically opens from £8,000. After we understand your goals, we'll recommend an approach that fits your budget.

How does payment work?

+
-

You get a fixed quote before we begin, so there are no surprise invoices mid-project. Payment is split into milestones tied to delivery — you approve progress before we move on.

What payment methods do you accept?

+
-

We accept bank transfers, Revolut and cryptocurrency payments when needed.

Do you provide invoices?

+
-

Yes. We provide invoices for our projects and services, with the payment details and information you need for your records.

Are there monthly fees after the project is done?

+
-

No. There are no mandatory monthly fees after launch. Hosting, domains, and any third-party tools you choose remain yours to manage. If you want ongoing design or development help later, we can set up optional support or a retainer — only when there’s a real reason to.

Who owns the website and design files?

+
-

You do. At the end of the project you receive the relevant files, access, and documentation to take ownership of what we've built. We don't want your site to become something you're afraid to touch or permanently dependent on us to manage.

Can I manage the website myself after launch?

+
-

Where the chosen platform allows it, we make sure you have the access and guidance needed to manage your website after launch. If you need us for future improvements, we're also available to help.

Will I be locked into working only with you?

+
-

No. We hand over accounts, credentials, and project files so another designer or developer can continue if you need them to. Need us again six months later? That’s fine too — we’re here when there’s a genuine reason to improve the site or product.

How involved do I need to be during the project?

+
-

You’ll always know where things stand. We share progress regularly and ask for feedback at clear decision points. You don’t need to micromanage — but your input on goals, content, and approvals keeps the work accurate and on schedule.

What if you're not the right fit?

+
-

We'll tell you. The free call is for clarity, not a hard sell. If another approach or supplier would serve you better, we'd rather say so early than take on the wrong project.

Do you offer support after launch?

+
-

Yes — as an option, not a requirement. Many projects include a short post-launch support window for fixes and questions. After that, ongoing updates or retainers are available if you want continuous improvements.
