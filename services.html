Premium 6-Step Process

Six clear steps so you always know where we are and what happens next. No black boxes.

Discovery & Alignment

We nail down your goals, who you're building for, and what's non-negotiable - so every design choice has a reason.

Research & Insight

We look at how people actually use your product (or your competitors') and where they get stuck - then we fix the stuff that matters most.

Structure & Flow

We map out the structure and paths users take so nothing feels confusing - they find what they need without thinking about it.

Interface & System

We build a visual system that stays consistent as you grow - same patterns, clear hierarchy, so every screen feels familiar.

Validation & Iteration

We test with real people and tweak until the rough edges are gone - so you're not debugging UX after launch.

Build & Launch

We hand over everything you need and stay involved so what goes live actually matches the design - no "it looked different in the file."

Common questions

Do I need to know exactly what service I need before contacting you?

+
-

No. If you're not sure whether you need UX, UI, a website redesign, development or something else, tell us what you're trying to achieve and we'll help you work out the right approach.

How long does a typical project take?

+
-

Depends on scope. A landing page takes 1-2 weeks. A full website 3-6 weeks. A SaaS product or complete brand system can take 2-4 months. We give you a clear timeline before we start.

Do you handle both design and development?

+
-

Yes. We work end-to-end — UX research, UI design, branding, and full web development. One team, one handoff, no gaps.

How much does a project cost?

+
-

Growth Systems and service packages on this page typically range from a few thousand pounds upward depending on scope. We give a fixed quote before starting so there are no surprises.

What payment methods do you accept?

+
-

We accept bank transfers, Revolut and cryptocurrency payments when needed.

Do you provide support after launch?

+
-

Yes — as an option, not a requirement. We can continue with updates or a retainer when there’s a real reason to. There are no mandatory monthly fees after launch.
