UX UI MATE is a UX/UI design studio in Newcastle upon Tyne. We help businesses without in-house design teams research, design and build better digital experiences — from straightforward business websites to more complex digital products.

Good businesses don't always need a design department.

Most businesses don't need a full-time UX designer, UI designer, developer and strategist.

They need the right people when they need them.

We work directly with businesses to understand what they're trying to achieve, identify what isn't working, design the right solution and help bring it to life.

How we work

Six clear steps so you always know where we are and what happens next.

01 — Understand

We learn about your business, your customers, your goals and what needs to change.

02 — Research

We look at the current experience, competitors, users and available evidence to understand where the real opportunities are.

03 — Plan

We define the structure, content, user journeys and priorities before visual design begins.

04 — Design

We turn the strategy into a clear, useful and distinctive digital experience.

05 — Build

We bring the approved design to life and make sure the finished product works as intended.

06 — Launch & handover

We launch the project and give you the access, files and guidance you need to understand and use what we've built.

Meet Alexander

Founder & UX/UI Designer

UX UI MATE was built around a simple idea: businesses shouldn't need to build an entire design department to get serious design work.

Alexander works directly with clients across research, UX, UI and digital design, bringing the thinking and execution together instead of passing projects through unnecessary layers of teams.

His approach is straightforward:

Understand the problem first. Challenge assumptions. Design with purpose. Build something that works in the real world.

Whether the project is a focused business website or a more complex digital product, clients work directly with the person responsible for the design.
