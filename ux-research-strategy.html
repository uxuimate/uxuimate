Quick guide

Why invest in UX research instead of improving the design directly?

+
-

UX research reveals how people actually interact with your product - what they understand, where they hesitate, and what causes friction. When design decisions are based on real behaviour instead of assumptions, you reduce rework and achieve more predictable outcomes.

Is UX research worth doing for a small business?

+
-

It depends on the project. A straightforward business website may only need a focused discovery process, while a more complex website, ecommerce experience or digital product may benefit from deeper research. We'll recommend an approach based on the actual problem rather than adding research for the sake of it.

How do you decide which research method to use?

+
-

We choose methods based on your goals, users, timeline and what we already know. A project may need interviews, usability testing, an audit, analytics or a combination of methods. We recommend the approach that answers the questions that matter most.

What will we actually receive?

+
-

Depending on the project, you may receive research findings, user needs and pain points, journey maps, usability findings, opportunity areas, prioritised recommendations and a clear set of next steps.

How long does UX research take?

+
-

Timelines depend on depth: quick audit 1-2 weeks, focused research 2-4 weeks, full discovery 4-8 weeks. We share insights as we uncover them, so you gain clarity early rather than waiting until the end.

Can UX research be done remotely?

+
-

Yes. Remote research is efficient, reliable, and allows us to reach a broader audience. It enables natural behaviour, faster scheduling, and full session recordings.

Have a question you need answered?

Tell us what you're trying to understand. We'll help you work out whether research is the right next step — and what level of research makes sense.
